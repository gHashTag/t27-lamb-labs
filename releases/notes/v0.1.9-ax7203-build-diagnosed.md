# v0.1.9-ax7203-build-diagnosed: chipdb verified, bitstream blocked

v0.1.9-ax7203-build-diagnosed: chipdb verified, bitstream blocked

Co-design iteration 6 builds the AX7203-native design and diagnoses why it does not yet produce
a bitstream.

CHIPDB: rebuilt from source and independently verified - size 333,207,792 B and md5
59f828c441e6e6081bfee63ff994660e both match the SSOT exactly.

DESIGN: 200 MHz differential clock (R4/T4), LEDs on the documented B13/C13 (the earlier shipped
bitstream used R23/T23, which the AX7203 routes elsewhere), UART TX on N15 emitting one byte per
done pulse for direct silicon token-interval timing.

TWO BLOCKERS, both precisely characterised:
- CLOCK SHORTFALL 3x: 65-73 MHz achieved against the board's 200 MHz, limited by the 32-bit
  accumulator feedback loop. The iteration-5 throughput at 200 MHz is therefore not achievable.
  Pipelining the accumulator is co-design iteration 7.
- OPENXC7 PACKER DEFECT: three distinct mis-packings, independent of branch, yosys version, ABC
  flow, placer, router and seed across 8 configurations. The FPGA flow is part of the design space.

No bitstream is claimed. The honest state is recorded.
