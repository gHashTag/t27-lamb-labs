v0.1.11-fmax-noise-audit: two frequency claims withdrawn by measurement

Co-design iteration 8 audits the metric that iterations 6 and 7 relied on.

The same byte-identical netlist measures 45.13, 48.98, 57.30, 71.86 and 73.15 MHz across five
seed/router combinations - a 62% spread. nextpnr's reported Fmax is placement noise on this
design, not a measure of logic depth.

Direct tests confirm it: deleting the KV read mux iteration 7 blamed gave 55.03 MHz (worse), the
BRAM version with no mux at all gave 71.86 MHz (identical), and the KV-read pipeline gave
40.52 MHz (worse).

WITHDRAWN: iteration 6's '3x clock shortfall' and iteration 7's 'the KV mux is the limiter'.
The design's achievable clock is UNKNOWN until Vivado or silicon says otherwise.

KEPT: every resource number, the quality metric, the combinational-vs-sequential comparison and
the lossless rule - all stable across seeds, and they are the portfolio's solid ground.

No tok/s or J/token figure may be derived from a nextpnr-reported Fmax.
