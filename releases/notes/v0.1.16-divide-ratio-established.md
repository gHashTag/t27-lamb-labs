v0.1.16-divide-ratio-established: R4/T4 measured at 398.3 MHz

The factor of two in the R4/T4 reading was settled experimentally before publishing.

The control is the measured R4/T4 design with CFGMCLK in place of the R4/T4 oscillator; the
netlists are cell-identical apart from the clock primitive and one LED tap. It reads 66.86 and
66.82 MHz, agreeing with the independently measured CFGMCLK of 67.16 MHz - so the Johnson chain
divides by 256, confirmed.

R4/T4 therefore measures 398.69 and 397.93 MHz -> published 398.3 MHz. The ratio
R4/T4 / CFGMCLK = 5.95-5.97 holds independently of any divide ratio.

The parent repository documents a 200 MHz oscillator on R4/T4. The measurement says about
twice that. The measurement is repeatable; the cause of the discrepancy is recorded as unknown.
