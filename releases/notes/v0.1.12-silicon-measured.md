# v0.1.12-silicon-measured: 67.16 MHz measured, and the ring oscillator withdrawn

v0.1.12-silicon-measured: 67.16 MHz measured, and the ring oscillator withdrawn

Co-design iteration 9 is the portfolio's first silicon measurements.

MEASURED CFGMCLK: 67.1583 MHz mean, 67.3927 median, 1.3325 stdev, n=9. Family nominal is
~66 MHz, so the board runs 1.8% above nominal. Method: a purpose-built BSCANE2 USER1 probe
with a prescaled ripple counter, read over the Digilent HS2.

MEASURED die telemetry: 44.681 C idle, 45.271 C with the design loaded (+0.590 C thermal
signature), VCCINT 0.99926 V, VCCAUX 1.80532 V. NOT a power measurement - the XADC has no
current sensor, so POWER_WATTS and J/token remain ESTIMATED.

The fabric ring oscillator appears NOT to oscillate: its counter was frozen over 20 reads
while the CFGMCLK control of the same probe counted correctly. The ~150 MHz estimate behind
every derived throughput figure is WITHDRAWN, and the shipped bitstream's 5.7 tok/s is now
SUSPECT because it clocks from the same ring oscillator.

Toolchain: "abc -lut 2" bypasses openXC7's LUT6 packing defect, which was the blocker that
stopped every previous build; a flip-flop ripple counter avoids the CARRY4 chain that
openXC7's placer splits.
